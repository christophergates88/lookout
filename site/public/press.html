About Lookout

In one sentence

Lookout brings police and fire scanners, live road cameras, crash reports, weather warnings, planes overhead and local news together in one iPhone app, for exactly where you are.

In a paragraph

Lookout is a free iPhone app that keeps watch on what's happening around you. It finds the live police, fire and EMS scanner feeds for your county, what drivers are reporting on the road, the public cameras closest to you, the planes overhead, recent earthquakes, local headlines and official National Weather Service warnings, all updated as you move. Its Drive mode turns the phone into a glanceable screen that reads new warnings aloud and shows the camera on the road ahead. Lookout has no accounts, ads or tracking. An optional subscription, Lookout Pro, adds live radar, Drive mode, background warning alerts and more.

Fact sheet

App name
Lookout: Scanner & Road Cams
Platform
iPhone, iOS 17 or later
Availability
Coming soon to the App Store in the United States. Works in every state.
Price
Free. Lookout Pro is $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year, with a free first week.
Lookout Pro adds
Drive mode, live NEXRAD radar, background warning alerts, up to 50 nearby cameras, 20 saved places and the daily storm risk
Free features
Scanner feeds, driver reports, public-safety tools, local news, planes, earthquakes, weather alerts, the forecast and the 3 nearest cameras
Data sources
National Weather Service, NOAA NEXRAD radar via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet, NOAA Storm Prediction Center, U.S. Geological Survey, adsb.lol, Broadcastify, OpenMHz, Waze, the GDELT Project, windy.com webcams, Apple Maps
Privacy
No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Saved places stay on the phone.
