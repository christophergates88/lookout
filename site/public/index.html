Questions, answered

Something else? Visit support or email hello@getlookoutapp.com.

Is Lookout free?

Yes. Scanner feeds, traffic reports, local news, planes and earthquakes, weather alerts for your spot, the map with traffic, the 3 nearest cameras and 1 saved place are free, with no account and no ads. Lookout Pro adds radar, Drive mode, background alerts, more cameras and places, and the daily storm risk for $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year, after a free week.

Can I listen to police and fire scanners?

Yes. The Scanner tab opens the live police, fire and EMS feeds for your state and county on Broadcastify, and recent dispatch calls you can replay on OpenMHz. Feeds are run by volunteers, so some counties don't have one.

Where does the information come from?

Forecasts and alerts come straight from the National Weather Service, and radar from NOAA's NEXRAD network via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet. Scanner audio is on Broadcastify and OpenMHz, driver reports on Waze, planes from adsb.lol, earthquakes from the U.S. Geological Survey, headlines from the GDELT Project, webcams from windy.com, and maps and traffic from Apple.

Does it work outside Georgia?

Yes, anywhere in the United States. Lookout uses your own location, so it works the same in every state. Radar covers the lower 48, and camera and scanner coverage is best around cities and highways.

Do I need an account?

No. Allow location once and Lookout loads the weather for wherever you are. There's nothing to sign up for.

What happens to my location?

Your phone sends your coordinates straight to the public weather, camera and flight services so they can return data for your spot. We don't run a server that collects it, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking. Saved places stay on your iPhone. The privacy policy has the details.

How do background alerts work?

With Lookout Pro, iOS wakes Lookout from time to time, usually every 15 minutes or more, to check for new warnings at your last location and your saved places. New ones arrive as notifications. iOS decides the timing, so keep Emergency Alerts turned on too.

Which cameras does Lookout show?

Public webcams from windy.com within about 75 miles of you, closest first. The free version shows the nearest 3, and Lookout Pro shows up to 50.

What iPhone do I need?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

How do I cancel Lookout Pro?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Lookout. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date and you won't be charged again.
