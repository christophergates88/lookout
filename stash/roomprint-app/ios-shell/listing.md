## Description
After a fire, storm or break-in, your insurer asks for a list of everything you lost, with values. Most people forget half of it, and receipts burn with everything else.

Roomprint fixes that in about twenty minutes.

• Tap a room, record it with your camera, done. The app guides you room by room.
• Snap the labels on your big-ticket items so brand, model and serial number are on record.
• Stop and come back any time. Your walkthrough is saved as you go.
• Within about a day you get an itemized record: every item by room, with an estimated replacement value, as a PDF and a spreadsheet you can hand to any insurer.

What you get
• Every room and outbuilding, including the garage, attic, shed, patio, dock and boat gear.
• Brand, model and serial number read from the labels you photograph.
• Estimated replacement cost, totaled by room.
• Proof for each item: each line points to the video moment or photo that shows it.
• A to-do list of what would make your record stronger.

Private and yours. Your uploads are used only to build your record, shared only with you, and raw video frames are deleted 30 days after delivery.

Roomprint is a service, not an appraisal and not insurance advice. Values are estimates of what a similar new item costs today.
