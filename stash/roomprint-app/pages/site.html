Why it matters

The claim is only as good as your list.

Insurers pay for what you can show you owned. Memory under stress is a poor list, and receipts burn with everything else.

"What brand was the TV? How big?"

You snap the label once. We record the model and serial number so you never have to remember.

"What was in the garage?"

Mowers, tools, freezers and kayaks add up fast and are the first things people forget.

"Do you have proof you owned it?"

Every item in your record points to the video moment or photo that shows it.

Questions

What people ask before they start.

Do I need an app?

No. Your private link works in Safari or Chrome on any recent iPhone or Android. If you like, add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app.

How long does it take me?

About 20 minutes for most homes, 30 to 40 for large ones with outbuildings. You can stop and come back; your link remembers where you were.

What if my video isn't good enough?

We'll email you if a room is too dark or too fast, and you can re-record just that room. Lights on, phone sideways, walk slowly, and pause for a couple of seconds on anything with a label.

Is this an appraisal?

No. Values are estimates of what similar new items cost today. For jewelry, art and collectibles we recommend a written appraisal, and we'll note which items need one.

Who sees my record?

Only you, unless you choose to share it. We don't sell or share your information, raw video frames are deleted 30 days after delivery, and we'll delete everything we hold whenever you ask.

Do I need to clean or organize first?

No. Just put away anything private, like bank papers, ID cards or safe combinations. Record your home as it is.

Will this lower my insurance price?

No. It isn't an insurance product and we don't give coverage advice. It helps you get paid fully and faster if you ever have a claim, and it gives you a real total to take to your insurance agent when you review your policy.

What if I buy new things?

Add a yearly update, or send us photos of big purchases and we'll add them to your record.
