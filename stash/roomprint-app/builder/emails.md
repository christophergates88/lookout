# Roomprint customer emails

Plain text, short, no marketing. Sender: the Gmail account connected to Claude. Sign every email
"Christopher Gates, Roomprint". Replace the {placeholders}. Never include the PDF password in the same
email as the download link unless the record is already unlocked (it is, by then).

## 1. Welcome / your private link  (sent by the builder's hourly run for homes started in the last day that have no welcome yet)
Subject: Your Roomprint walkthrough link

Hi {first name},

Here's your private walkthrough link for {home name}:
{walkthroughUrl}

Keep it to yourself; anyone with the link can see your walkthrough. Open it on your phone, tap a room, record it, and come back any time. When you're done, tap "Send to Roomprint" and we'll build your record, usually within a day.

Tips: lights on, phone sideways, walk slowly, open closets and drawers, and pause on labels.

Christopher Gates, Roomprint
