**Description:**
Lookout keeps watch on what's happening around you: police and fire scanners, live road cameras, crash and hazard reports, weather warnings, planes overhead, earthquakes and local news, all for exactly where you are, updated as you move.

FREE
• Scanner: live police, fire and EMS radio for your state and county on Broadcastify, and recent dispatch calls on OpenMHz
• Traffic reports: crashes, police and hazards reported by drivers on Waze, plus your state's 511 road conditions
• Public safety: Citizen 911 incident reports in larger metro areas, crime maps, power outages, wildfires and county emergency info
• Local news: recent stories that name your town
• Map with live Apple traffic, planes overhead and recent earthquakes
• National Weather Service alerts for your exact location, with the warning area drawn on the map
• Current conditions, the next 12 hours and the 7-day forecast
• The nearest public cameras around you
• One saved place (home, work, school) with its alerts and temperature

LOOKOUT PRO
• Drive mode: a big, glanceable screen with your speed and heading, new alerts read aloud, and the nearest camera ahead on your route
• Live NEXRAD radar over your map
• Background warning alerts for your location and saved places
• Every public camera nearby, closest first
• Up to 20 saved places
• Today's severe-storm risk from the Storm Prediction Center

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your saved places stay on your phone.

Lookout Pro is $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year after a 1-week free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID's Subscriptions settings.

Lookout is a helper, not an official warning system. Keep Emergency Alerts turned on and follow your local officials. Scanner feeds are run by volunteers and aren't available in every county. Forecasts and alerts come from the National Weather Service and cover the United States. Webcams provided by windy.com. Aircraft data from adsb.lol. Earthquakes from the U.S. Geological Survey.
