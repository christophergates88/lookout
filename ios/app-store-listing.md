**Promotional text (170 max):**

**Description:**
Lookout shows you what's happening right where you are: weather, official warnings, radar and live public cameras, updated as you move.

FREE
• Current conditions, the next 12 hours and the 7-day forecast from the National Weather Service
• Active weather alerts for your exact location, with the warning area drawn on the map
• Map with live Apple traffic
• The nearest public cameras around you
• One saved place (home, work, daycare) with its alerts and temperature

LOOKOUT PRO
• Drive mode: a big, glanceable screen with your speed and heading, new alerts read aloud, and the nearest camera ahead on your route
• Live NEXRAD radar over your map
• Background warning alerts for your location and saved places
• Every public camera nearby, closest first
• Up to 20 saved places
• Today's severe-storm risk from the Storm Prediction Center

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your saved places stay on your phone.

Lookout Pro is $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year after a 1-week free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID's Subscriptions settings.

Lookout is a helper, not an official warning system. Keep Emergency Alerts turned on and follow your local officials. Forecasts and United States coverage come from the National Weather Service. Webcams provided by windy.com.
